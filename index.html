From the pipeline trench to the workflow engine

I'm a mechanical project engineer based in Kampala. My work covers oil and gas quality assurance, renewable energy, HVAC/MEP design and industrial CAD, and I have delivered projects for some of East Africa's best-known organisations.

On EACOP I inspect incoming pipe, run hydrostatic tests and manage non-conformance reports. At Zenith Infrastructure I managed sites and teams, and designed systems in SolidWorks, AutoCAD MEP and Revit MEP before supervising their fabrication and installation.

I also build AI and automation systems with Python, n8n and Make.com, and I take on freelance automation work for businesses that want to save time on repetitive tasks.
